# Feature Specification: Shopping List

**Feature Branch**: `004-shopping-list`

**Created**: 2026-11-17

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: User description: "branch 003 is merged go to next thing". The user picked the **Shopping List** from docs/SPEC.md as the next feature (2026-11-17).

Domain terms in **bold** are defined in [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md). The product contract is [docs/SPEC.md](../../docs/SPEC.md) ("Shopping List", "Screens → Lista zakupów"). This feature builds on [feature 001](../001-shell-products-dishes/spec.md) (Products) and [feature 002](../002-week-planning/spec.md) (Meals, Servings scaling, Linked and Detached Meals, copying to the next Week). It replaces the placeholder in the Lista zakupów tab.

## Background

The app's first purpose is to let one person shop once a week and buy exactly what the plan needs. The planned **Week** already holds every **Meal** with its **Servings**. This feature turns that plan into the **Shopping List**: one line per **Product** with the amount the plan needs. Before shopping, the user marks what they already have as **At Home** with one tap, without saying how much. Where they want to buy a different amount than the plan needs, they tap the amount and type the one they want (an **Adjustment**). They add things the plan doesn't know about (an **Extra**), tick lines off in the shop, and share what is left as text.

The list is never generated by hand. It is always the current plan, with the Week's Adjustments, At Home marks, Extras and ticks applied on top.

Words used in this spec (UI terms, not domain terms):

- **List item**: one line of the Shopping List. It is either a **Product item** (from the plan) or an **Extra**.
- **Planned amount**: the sum, over all Meals of the Week, of that Product's scaled Ingredient amount (`ingredient.amount / servingsYield × servings`, from the Meal's Dish or, for a Detached Meal, from its own copy).
- **Amount to buy**: the Adjustment's amount if the Product item has one that applies, otherwise the Planned amount.
- **Tick**: the "bought" mark the user sets in the shop. docs/SPEC.md calls it the checked state.
- **Shown Week**: the Week the Lista zakupów tab is showing.

## Clarifications

### Session 2026-11-17

- Q: Which Week does Lista zakupów show, and how does the user change it? → A: The list has its own Week navigation (← / → and a swipe) and opens on the current Week. It does not follow the Week viewed in Tydzień.
- Q: What does "Udostępnij listę" include? → A: Only what is still to buy: unticked Product items that aren't At Home, and unticked Extras.
- Q: Where does a struck-through item sit in the list? → A: At the bottom, sorted together with the ticked items, so the top part holds only what is still to buy. (Asked when strike-through meant "Total at zero or below". After the answer below, strike-through means At Home, and the answer applies to At Home items.)
- Q: How does a ticked item look, given that strike-through has its own meaning? → A: A filled tick and dimmed text, never struck through.
- Q: What should a struck-through item show as its amount? → A: The user replaced amount-based Adjustments. (1) Having a Product at home is a separate yes/no mark, **At Home**, with no amount: the item is crossed out, dimmed and moved to the bottom. It is separate from the tick and has no text label. (2) To buy a different amount, the user taps the item's amount and types the amount to buy (e.g. 500 g where the plan needs 400 g). The item shows the typed amount, styled as more or less than planned, with the Planned amount below it. There are no plus or minus amounts. docs/SPEC.md and GLOSSARY.md were updated to match.
- Q: The user typed 500 g where the plan needed 400 g, and the plan then changes to 600 g. What does the item show? → A: It resets to the plan: 600 g, unstyled. Any change to the Product's Planned amount discards the typed amount.
- Q: How does the user mark an item At Home? → A: With a second, small button on each Product item, to the right of the amount (a house icon). The tick on the left stays "bought", and tapping the amount opens the amount field.

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - See what to buy for the Week (Priority: P1)

The user opens the **Lista zakupów** tab and sees every Product the Week's Meals need, once each, with the Planned amount in the Product's unit. Amounts read the way a shop sells them: 1500 g shows as 1,5 kg, and pieces are rounded up to whole numbers. When the user changes the plan in Tydzień, the list follows without any action on their part.

**Why this priority**: This is the reason the app exists. The other stories mark, change, tick off or share this list, and none of them is useful without it.

**Independent Test**: Plan a Week with a few Dishes that share Products (one Dish in two Meals, one Meal at 1,5 Servings, one Detached Meal with a changed amount), open Lista zakupów, and confirm every amount equals a hand calculation. Then change a Meal's Servings in Tydzień and confirm the list shows the new amount.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** Dish "Owsianka" yields 2 Servings with 100 g "płatki owsiane" and 300 ml "mleko", and the Week holds "Owsianka" at 1 Serving on Monday and at 3 Servings on Wednesday, **When** the user opens Lista zakupów, **Then** it shows "mleko 600 ml" and "płatki owsiane 200 g", each once.
2. **Given** the same Week with a second Dish that uses 500 ml "mleko", **When** the user opens the list, **Then** "mleko" shows once, as "1,1 l".
3. **Given** Meals that need 2,5 pcs "jajka" in total, **When** the user opens the list, **Then** it shows "jajka 3 szt.".
4. **Given** a Detached Meal whose copy uses 80 g "płatki owsiane" per Serving where its Dish uses 50 g, **When** the user opens the list, **Then** that Meal adds its own 80 g per Serving, not the Dish's 50 g.
5. **Given** a Meal whose Dish has since become an **Archived Dish**, **When** the user opens the list, **Then** that Meal's Ingredients are still counted.
6. **Given** the list is open on the Shown Week, **When** the user goes to Tydzień, sets a Meal of that Week from 1 to 2 Servings and comes back, **Then** that Meal's Products show the higher amounts.
7. **Given** Products "cebula", "Awokado", "łosoś" and "ziemniaki" on the list, **When** the user opens the list, **Then** they appear as "Awokado", "cebula", "łosoś", "ziemniaki" (alphabetical, ignoring case, with Polish letters in their Polish places).
8. **Given** a Week with no Meals and no Extras, **When** the user opens the list, **Then** it shows a short message that there is nothing to buy for this Week yet.
9. **Given** today is Tuesday 2026-11-17, **When** the user opens the app and goes to Lista zakupów, **Then** it shows the list for "16–22 lis 2026", whatever Week Tydzień is showing.
10. **Given** the list for 16–22 lis 2026, **When** the user taps → or swipes the Week header to the left, **Then** the list for "23–29 lis 2026" is shown. ← or a swipe to the right goes back a Week.
11. **Given** Tydzień shows 30 lis – 6 gru 2026 and Lista zakupów shows 23–29 lis 2026, **When** the user switches between the two tabs, **Then** each keeps its own Week.

### User Story 2 - Tick items off while shopping (Priority: P1)

In the shop the user ticks each List item as it goes into the basket. Ticked items are dimmed and move to the bottom, so what is still to buy stays at the top. Ticks belong to that Week and survive closing the app.

**Why this priority**: The list is used in the shop, one-handed, often offline. Without ticks the user has to remember what is already in the basket, which defeats the purpose of one planned trip.

**Independent Test**: Tick three items on a list of ten, close and reopen the app offline, and confirm the same three are ticked and sit at the bottom. Then change the plan and confirm the ticks are still there.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a list with "cebula", "mleko" and "ziemniaki", **When** the user ticks "cebula", **Then** "cebula" shows a filled tick and dimmed text (not crossed out) and moves below "mleko" and "ziemniaki".
2. **Given** "cebula" is ticked, **When** the user unticks it, **Then** it returns to its alphabetical place among the items still to buy.
3. **Given** several ticked items and an At Home "ryż", **When** the user views the list, **Then** the ticked items and "ryż" form the bottom part, alphabetical among themselves, below every item still to buy.
4. **Given** ticked items, **When** the user closes the app and opens it again with no network, **Then** the same items are ticked.
5. **Given** "mleko" is ticked, **When** the user adds a Meal that also uses "mleko", **Then** "mleko" shows the new amount and stays ticked.
6. **Given** "mleko" is ticked in one Week, **When** the user views the next Week's list, **Then** "mleko" is not ticked there.

### User Story 3 - Mark what is already at home (Priority: P2)

Before shopping, the user goes through the list at home. They have rice and onions, so they tap the house button on "ryż" and "cebula". Each is crossed out, dimmed and moves to the bottom. They don't say how much they have; the app doesn't need to know.

**Why this priority**: Buying exactly what is needed means skipping what is already in the kitchen, and it has to be quick or it won't be done. The list is still usable without it (the user can skip a line mentally), so it ranks below seeing and ticking.

**Independent Test**: Mark three Product items At Home, confirm they are crossed out, dimmed and at the bottom, close and reopen the app, and confirm they still are. Unmark one and confirm it returns to the items still to buy.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the list shows "ryż 1 kg" among the items still to buy, **When** the user taps the house button on "ryż", **Then** "ryż" is crossed out and dimmed, moves to the bottom part, and its house button shows as active. No amount is asked for.
2. **Given** "ryż" is At Home, **When** the user taps its house button again, **Then** it is no longer crossed out or dimmed and returns to its alphabetical place among the items still to buy.
3. **Given** "ryż" is At Home, **When** the plan changes the amount of "ryż" needed, **Then** "ryż" stays At Home.
4. **Given** "ryż" is At Home in one Week, **When** the user views the next Week's list, **Then** "ryż" is not At Home there.
5. **Given** an Extra "kawa", **When** the user views it, **Then** it has no house button (an Extra the user already has is simply deleted).
6. **Given** "ryż" is At Home, **When** the user taps its tick, **Then** it is also ticked. It stays crossed out and dimmed in the bottom part.

### User Story 4 - Buy a different amount than the plan needs (Priority: P2)

The plan needs 400 g of rice, but the user wants a bit extra, so they tap "400 g" on the rice item and type 500. The item now shows 500 g, styled to show it is more than planned, with "400 g" from the plan below it. The same works for buying less. If the plan later changes how much rice it needs, the typed amount is dropped and the item shows the plan's amount again.

**Why this priority**: Package sizes, stocking up and partial leftovers mean the amount bought often differs from the plan. Without it the user can still buy a different amount from memory, so it ranks with marking At Home.

**Independent Test**: Plan a Week that needs 400 g "ryż", tap the amount, type 500 and save, and confirm the item shows 500 g styled as more, with 400 g below. Then add a Meal that uses rice and confirm the item shows the new Planned amount with no styling.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the list shows "ryż 400 g", **When** the user taps "400 g", **Then** a field opens for the amount to buy in grams, filled with 400.
2. **Given** that field, **When** the user types 500 and saves, **Then** the item shows "500 g" styled as more than planned, and "400 g" (the Planned amount) below it.
3. **Given** that field, **When** the user types 250 and saves, **Then** the item shows "250 g" styled as less than planned, and "400 g" below it.
4. **Given** "ryż" shows 500 g typed over 400 g planned, **When** the user adds a Meal that raises the Planned amount to 600 g, **Then** the item shows "600 g" with no styling and no planned amount below: the typed amount is gone.
5. **Given** "ryż" shows 500 g typed over 400 g planned, **When** the user opens the field, clears it or types 400, and saves, **Then** the item shows "400 g" with no styling.
6. **Given** the field, **When** the user saves without changing the filled-in value, **Then** nothing changes.
7. **Given** the field, **When** the user types 0, a negative number or text that is not a number, **Then** saving is blocked and the field is pointed out.
8. **Given** "jajka" planned at 2,5 pcs ("3 szt."), **When** the user types 6 and saves, **Then** the item shows "6 szt." styled as more than planned, with "3 szt." below it.
9. **Given** "ryż" with a typed 500 g, **When** the user ticks it or marks it At Home, **Then** the typed amount is kept and still shown.

### User Story 5 - Add things the plan doesn't cover (Priority: P2)

The user also needs toilet paper and coffee, which no Dish uses. They type "papier toaletowy" into the list and it appears as an **Extra**: just the text, with no amount or unit. Extras are ticked like any other item and belong to that Week.

**Why this priority**: One trip a week only works if the list holds everything to buy, not just food from the plan. It is independent of the other P2 stories and ranks with them.

**Independent Test**: Add two Extras, tick one, change the plan, close and reopen the app, and confirm both Extras are there with the same tick state. Delete one and confirm it is gone.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the list, **When** the user types "papier toaletowy" in the field for new Extras and confirms, **Then** "papier toaletowy" appears on the list in its alphabetical place, with no amount, and the field is empty again.
2. **Given** the field for new Extras, **When** the user confirms it empty or with only spaces, **Then** nothing is added.
3. **Given** an Extra "kawa", **When** the user ticks it, **Then** it moves to the bottom part like a ticked Product item.
4. **Given** an Extra "kawa", **When** the user swipes it and deletes it, **Then** it disappears from the list at once, without a confirmation.
5. **Given** an Extra "kawa" in one Week, **When** the user views another Week's list, **Then** "kawa" is not on it.
6. **Given** Extras in the Shown Week, **When** the user changes the plan or copies the previous Week into it with "Skopiuj do następnego tygodnia", **Then** the Extras are unchanged.

### User Story 6 - Share the list (Priority: P3)

The user taps "Udostępnij listę" and sends what is left to buy as plain text, e.g. to the other person in the household, who adds the lines to their own list by hand. Where the device can't share, the text is copied to the clipboard instead.

**Why this priority**: Two people use two separate installs (single-user app), so sharing text is the only way to merge their lists. It is useful, but the list works for one person without it.

**Independent Test**: With a list holding a ticked item, an At Home item, an item with a typed amount and an Extra, tap "Udostępnij listę", send the text to a notes app, and confirm it contains the Week's dates and only the items still to buy, with readable amounts. On a browser without sharing, confirm the same text is in the clipboard and a short message says so.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a list for 16–22 lis 2026, **When** the user taps "Udostępnij listę" on a device that can share, **Then** the system share sheet opens with plain text starting with a heading naming the list and the Week's dates, followed by one line per item still to buy.
2. **Given** the shared text, **When** the user reads it, **Then** each Product item reads as its name and its Amount to buy in the same units as on screen (e.g. "mleko 1,1 l", "jajka 3 szt.", "ryż 500 g" for a typed 500 g), and each Extra as its text.
3. **Given** the list has ticked and At Home items, **When** the user shares it, **Then** those items are left out.
4. **Given** a browser that cannot share, **When** the user taps "Udostępnij listę", **Then** the same text is copied to the clipboard and a short message confirms it.
5. **Given** the user cancels the system share sheet, **When** it closes, **Then** nothing changes and no error is shown.
6. **Given** a Week with nothing left to buy (no items, or every item ticked or At Home), **When** the user views the list, **Then** "Udostępnij listę" is not offered.

### Edge Cases

- **Same Product in many Meals and Dishes**: it is one line with the summed amount, however many Meals, Dishes or Detached copies it comes from.
- **Rounding**: the Planned amount is computed from unrounded Meal amounts and rounded once, for display. Grams and millilitres show as whole numbers below 1000 (74,6 g → 75 g). From 1000 up they show as kg or l with up to 2 decimals and no trailing zeros (1250 g → 1,25 kg; 2000 g → 2 kg; 1234 g → 1,23 kg). Pieces are rounded up to a whole number (2,01 → 3). A typed amount is shown by the same rules.
- **Near the 1000 boundary**: the unit switches on the rounded value, so 999,6 g shows as "1 kg", not "1000 g".
- **Tiny amounts**: a Planned amount above zero that would show as "0 g" after rounding (e.g. 0,3 g) is shown as "1 g", so a needed item never reads as zero.
- **What counts as a plan change for an Adjustment**: anything that changes the Product's Planned amount in that Week: adding, removing or re-serving a Meal, detaching or restoring a Meal, editing a Dish used by a Linked Meal, merging Products, or copying the previous Week over this one. A change elsewhere in the Week that leaves this Product's Planned amount the same keeps the typed amount.
- **Plan changes back**: the typed amount belongs to the Planned amount it was typed against. If the plan moves away and later returns to exactly that Planned amount, the typed amount applies again. (The user sees no difference unless they undo a plan change by hand, and this keeps the list derived from stored data.)
- **Filled-in value**: the amount field is filled with the current Amount to buy in the Product's unit, rounded as on screen. Saving it unchanged does nothing, so a rounded Planned amount (e.g. 1234,5 g filled as 1235) never turns into a typed amount by accident.
- **Typed amount equal to the plan**: saving the Planned amount (to 2 decimals) removes the typed amount, as clearing the field does.
- **Product renamed**: the line shows the new name at once and keeps its Adjustment, At Home mark and tick.
- **Products merged** (Produkty): the merged Product's line joins the kept Product's line. Typed amounts on either are dropped, because the joined Planned amount changes. In each Week, the joined line keeps the tick (or At Home mark) only if every one of the two Products that was on that Week's list had it, so nothing still to buy is hidden.
- **Product deleted**: only Products used by no Dish or Meal can be deleted (feature 001). Any Adjustment, At Home mark or tick it still has in any Week is removed with it.
- **Product unit changed**: allowed only on unused Products (feature 001). Its Adjustments are removed, because their amounts were in the old unit. Its ticks and At Home marks are kept.
- **Product leaves the plan**: its line disappears. If it comes back later in the same Week, its earlier tick and At Home mark come back with it. A typed amount applies only if the Planned amount is the same as when it was typed.
- **Meal moved between Weeks** (desktop drag and drop, later feature) or copied with "Skopiuj do następnego tygodnia": both Weeks' lists follow their own Meals. The target Week's Extras, ticks and At Home marks are left as they are (feature 002, FR-033). Its typed amounts follow the rule above.
- **A Product with a zero amount**: Ingredient amounts are always above zero (feature 001), so every planned Product contributes something.
- **Large Week**: 35 Meals over 100 distinct Products is a normal Week and must not feel slow (SC-002).
- **Very long names and Extras**: they wrap onto further lines rather than being cut off, and the amount and buttons stay readable and tappable.
- **Duplicate Extras**: allowed. "kawa" can be added twice; they are separate Extras.
- **An Extra with the same name as a Product item**: allowed. They are separate lines.
- **Offline**: everything in this feature works without a network, including sharing to apps on the device and copying to the clipboard.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

**The list**

- **FR-001**: The Lista zakupów tab MUST show the Shopping List of the Shown Week, replacing the current placeholder.
- **FR-002**: The tab MUST have its own Week header naming the Shown Week's dates (e.g. "16–22 lis 2026"), with ← / → buttons that show the previous and next Week. On phones, a horizontal swipe on the header MUST also change the Week: left for the next Week, right for the previous one, applied on release. The buttons stay visible on every screen size.
- **FR-003**: When the app starts, the Shown Week MUST be the Week containing today. While the app runs, the tab MUST keep its Shown Week when the user moves to other tabs and back. It MUST NOT follow or change the Week viewed in Tydzień.
- **FR-004**: The list MUST contain one Product item for each Product with a Planned amount above zero in the Shown Week, and one line for each Extra of the Shown Week.
- **FR-005**: The Planned amount of a Product MUST be the sum, over all Meals dated Monday to Sunday of the Shown Week, of that Product's Ingredient amount divided by the Meal's servings yield and multiplied by the Meal's Servings. For a Linked Meal the Ingredients and yield come from its Dish (including an Archived Dish); for a Detached Meal from its own copy.
- **FR-006**: The list MUST be live: any change to the Shown Week's Meals, to a Dish used by a Linked Meal in it, or to a Product, MUST be reflected the next time the list is visible, with no action from the user, and at once if the list is already on screen.
- **FR-007**: Each Product item MUST show, from left to right: its tick control, the Product's name, its Amount to buy, and its At Home button. When an Adjustment applies, the amount MUST be styled to show whether it is more or less than planned, and the Planned amount MUST be shown below it.
- **FR-008**: Amounts MUST be formatted as follows, with a comma as the decimal separator:
  - g: below 1000 as whole grams ("750 g"); from 1000 as kg with up to 2 decimals and no trailing zeros ("1,25 kg").
  - ml: the same rule with ml and l.
  - pcs: rounded up to a whole number, labelled "szt." ("3 szt.").
  - The kg or l switch uses the rounded value (999,6 g → "1 kg").
  - An amount above zero that rounds to 0 g or 0 ml shows as "1 g" or "1 ml".
- **FR-009**: The list MUST have two parts. The top part holds the items still to buy: Product items that are neither ticked nor At Home, and unticked Extras. The bottom part holds every ticked or At Home item. Each part MUST be sorted alphabetically by the text shown (Product name or Extra text), ignoring case and using Polish alphabetical order. Extras MUST be sorted together with Product items, not in a separate section.
- **FR-010**: When the Shown Week has no Product items and no Extras, the tab MUST show a short message that there is nothing to buy for this Week yet, and the field for adding Extras.

**Ticks**

- **FR-011**: Each List item MUST have a tick control that ticks or unticks it with one tap.
- **FR-012**: A ticked item MUST show a filled tick and dimmed text. It MUST NOT be crossed out, which is reserved for At Home (FR-015).
- **FR-013**: Ticks MUST be stored per Week: a Product item's tick belongs to that Product in that Week, and an Extra's tick to that Extra. Ticks MUST be kept when the plan changes.

**At Home**

- **FR-014**: Each Product item MUST have an At Home button (a house icon, no text) to the right of its amount, which marks or unmarks the Product as At Home for the Shown Week with one tap. Extras MUST NOT have one.
- **FR-015**: An At Home item MUST be shown crossed out and dimmed, with its At Home button shown as active, and sit in the bottom part of the list (FR-009). Setting it MUST NOT ask for an amount.
- **FR-016**: At Home marks MUST be stored per Week and Product, kept when the plan changes, and independent of the tick: an item can be ticked, At Home, both or neither.

**Adjustments**

- **FR-017**: Tapping a Product item's amount MUST open a field for the amount to buy, in the Product's unit, filled with the current Amount to buy rounded as on screen. The field MUST also show the Planned amount.
- **FR-018**: The field MUST accept a number above zero (comma or dot as separator), stored rounded to 2 decimals; more decimals are rounded, not rejected. Saving MUST:
  - change nothing when the value is the one it was filled with;
  - remove the Adjustment when the field is empty or the value equals the Planned amount (to 2 decimals);
  - otherwise set the Adjustment to the typed amount, replacing any earlier one.
- **FR-019**: Saving MUST be blocked, with the field pointed out, when the value is 0, negative, or not a number.
- **FR-020**: An Adjustment MUST record the Planned amount it was typed against, and MUST apply only while the Product's Planned amount in that Week equals it (to 2 decimals). While it doesn't apply, the item MUST show the Planned amount with no styling.
- **FR-021**: A Product MUST have at most one Adjustment per Week. Adjustments MUST NOT affect any other Week, and MUST be kept when the item is ticked or marked At Home.

**Extras**

- **FR-022**: The tab MUST offer a field for adding an Extra to the Shown Week. Confirming it MUST add an Extra with the typed text, trimmed of leading and trailing spaces and with runs of inner spaces collapsed to one, and clear the field. Empty or whitespace-only text MUST NOT add anything.
- **FR-023**: The user MUST be able to delete an Extra by swiping it. Deleting MUST NOT ask for confirmation.
- **FR-024**: Extras MUST belong to one Week and MUST NOT be changed by plan changes or by "Skopiuj do następnego tygodnia". Extras have no amount, no Adjustment and no At Home mark.

**Sharing**

- **FR-025**: The tab MUST offer "Udostępnij listę" whenever the top part of the list (items still to buy) is not empty.
- **FR-026**: "Udostępnij listę" MUST produce plain text: a first line naming the list and the Shown Week's dates, then one line per item in the top part of the list, in the order shown on screen. A Product item's line is its name and Amount to buy formatted as in FR-008, without the Planned amount; an Extra's line is its text. Ticked and At Home items MUST NOT be included.
- **FR-027**: The text MUST be passed to the device's share sheet where available. Otherwise it MUST be copied to the clipboard and a short message MUST confirm the copy. Cancelling the share sheet MUST NOT show an error.

**Interaction with other features**

- **FR-028**: Merging Product A into Product B (Produkty) MUST, in every Week, remove the Adjustments of A and B, and keep B's tick and At Home mark only if every one of A and B with a Planned amount above zero in that Week had it. A's own ticks and At Home marks MUST then be removed.
- **FR-029**: Deleting a Product MUST remove its Adjustments, ticks and At Home marks in every Week.
- **FR-030**: Changing a Product's unit MUST remove its Adjustments in every Week, and keep its ticks and At Home marks.
- **FR-031**: "Skopiuj do następnego tygodnia" MUST continue to leave the next Week's Extras, ticks and At Home marks unchanged (feature 002, FR-033). Its Adjustments follow FR-020.

**General**

- **FR-032**: Adjustments, At Home marks, Extras and ticks MUST be stored on the device, survive closing and reopening the app, and work with no network.
- **FR-033**: All user-facing text MUST be in Polish. Framework7's own built-in labels stay as shipped.
- **FR-034**: On desktop (about 1024 px and wider) the tab MUST use the shared content column (feature 002, FR-041), and any sheet it opens MUST line up with it like the app's other sheets.

### Key Entities

- **Shopping List**: derived, never stored. For one Week: the Product items computed from that Week's Meals, with their Adjustments, At Home marks and ticks, plus that Week's Extras with their ticks.
- **Product item**: derived. A Product, its Planned amount, its Adjustment if one applies, its Amount to buy, and its tick and At Home state for the Week.
- **Adjustment**: stored. A Week, a Product, the amount to buy the user typed, and the Planned amount it was typed against. At most one per Product per Week.
- **At Home mark**: stored. A Week and a Product the user already has at home. Kept when the Product leaves the plan.
- **Extra**: stored. A Week, free text and a tick state. Has its own identity, so duplicates are separate.
- **Product tick**: stored. A Week and a Product that is ticked on that Week's list. Kept when the Product leaves the plan.
- **Week** (existing): identified by its Monday. Owns the stored kinds above.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: For a reference Week of at least 20 Meals (Linked and Detached, fractional Servings, a Product shared by several Dishes, every unit), 100% of the list's amounts match a hand calculation of the rules in FR-005 and FR-008.
- **SC-002**: With 35 Meals over 100 distinct Products in the Week and a year of Weeks stored, the list appears in under 300 ms after the tab is opened, as perceived by the user.
- **SC-003**: After any plan change in Tydzień, the list is correct the first time it is seen again, in 100% of cases, with no manual refresh. Extras, ticks and At Home marks are unchanged, and typed amounts are kept exactly for the Products whose Planned amount did not change.
- **SC-004**: Marking an item At Home or ticking it takes one tap and no typing. Going through a 30-item list at home and marking 10 items At Home takes 10 taps.
- **SC-005**: While the user ticks a 30-item list in the shop, an item still to buy never leaves the top part of the list.
- **SC-006**: A shared list can be read by another person and turned into their own list by hand without asking what any line means: every line has a name and, for Product items, an amount with a unit.
- **SC-007**: After one online load, 100% of this feature's functions work in airplane mode, including after the app is closed and restarted.
- **SC-008**: No Extra, tick or At Home mark is ever lost or moved to another Week by plan changes, copying a Week, Product renames or app restarts.

## Assumptions

- **Feature scope**: this feature covers docs/SPEC.md's "Shopping List" section and the Lista zakupów screen: the live list, At Home marks, Adjustments, Extras, ticks, display rules and "Udostępnij listę". Goals and summaries, backup export/import and the desktop Tydzień grid stay in later features.
- **Simple Week swipe on the list**: the list's Week header changes Week when the swipe is released, like Tydzień before feature 003. The follow-the-finger gesture from feature 003 is not repeated here, because the list is not the app's primary navigation and its rows have their own swipe (deleting an Extra). The ← / → buttons stay visible on phones for the same reason.
- **No jump to today**: as in Tydzień, there is no "back to current Week" control. The list opens on the current Week at each app start (FR-003).
- **Adjustments only on listed Products**: an amount can be typed only on a Product item, so only for a Product the plan needs. To buy something the plan doesn't use, the user adds an Extra.
- **Typed amounts are absolute and short-lived**: the user types the amount to buy, not a difference. Because it is tied to the Planned amount it was typed against (FR-020), a plan change never leaves a stale amount on the list.
- **Ticks and At Home marks are kept when amounts change**: docs/SPEC.md says checks are kept when the plan changes, and At Home follows the same rule. The user is expected to notice a changed amount on a ticked item.
- **Merging marks conservatively**: after a Product merge, the joined line is ticked or At Home only if both Products that were on the list had the mark, because an unmarked line means something may still need buying.
- **Unit change drops Adjustments**: an amount typed as 500 g makes no sense as 500 ml. This can only happen on an unused Product.
- **No editing of Extras**: an Extra is deleted and added again to change it. docs/SPEC.md only asks for free-text items, and editing would add a sheet for a rare need.
- **No confirmation when deleting an Extra**: it is one line of text and quick to add again.
- **The amount field is in the Product's base unit**: grams, millilitres or pieces, even when the list shows kg or l. The user types 1500 for 1,5 kg.
- **UI labels**: the Polish labels for the amount field, the Extras field, the empty state and the house button's accessible name are chosen in the plan's UI contract. The At Home button has no visible text, at the user's request.
- **Backup**: backup export/import (a later feature) must include Adjustments, At Home marks, Extras and ticks, since they are user data that cannot be recomputed.
- **Phones and iPhone first**: checked on a real iPhone, as the installed app and in a Safari tab. The Android check stays deferred, as in feature 001.
- **Framework7 patterns**: the list, ticks, field, swipe-to-delete and buttons use Framework7's standard parts in the app's look (green accent, warm background, white cards).
